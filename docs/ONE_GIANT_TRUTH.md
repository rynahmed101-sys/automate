# Automate's One Giant Truth

> **Everything may be questioned. Nothing is automatically believed. Nothing is automatically dismissed merely for being unconventional.**

This is a foundational principle of Automate.

Automate does not treat established mathematics, physical theories, software libraries, AI proposals, experimental observations, or its own previous conclusions as unquestionable authority.

A claim may be:

- derived from an established formal system;
- computed by a mathematical or numerical engine;
- supported by experiment or observation;
- reproduced independently;
- contradicted by another method;
- internally consistent but physically unsupported;
- novel and unconventional;
- unresolved.

Automate's responsibility is not to decide which ideas are respectable before examining them. Its responsibility is to make the **basis, assumptions, derivation, computation, observations, contradictions, and unresolved parts of a claim inspectable**.

Established methods are instruments, not authorities.

Unconventional ideas are hypotheses, not truths merely because they are unconventional.

The system must preserve both freedoms:

1. **Freedom to question the established.**
2. **Freedom to question the new.**

Therefore, Automate must never use verification as a mechanism for conformity. Verification exists to expose what follows, what does not follow, what was observed, what was assumed, what failed, and what remains unknown.

> **Question freely. Claim carefully. Show what happened.**

This principle does not replace mathematical proof, physical evidence, or formal verification. It defines how Automate relates to them: as distinct forms of inquiry whose meaning must remain explicit rather than being collapsed into a single hierarchy of “truth.”

## Architectural consequence

Automate may use established mathematical and scientific software as computational instruments and independent comparison paths. Their outputs are inputs to Automate's reasoning and evidence model, not unquestionable declarations of truth.

Likewise, an AI-generated proposal may enter the system without being rejected merely because it is unconventional. It must instead encounter the same discipline of derivation, testing, contradiction search, assumptions, and reproducibility available to every other claim.

The objective is not to build a machine that merely reproduces the existing hierarchy of knowledge.

The objective is to build a machine that can **explore beyond that hierarchy without losing intellectual honesty**.
